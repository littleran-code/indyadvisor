How the process works

Three conversations, in order. Each one builds on the last, and you can stop after any of them.

1. Where you want to be

The first meeting is about your goal, not your portfolio. What does "on track" actually look like for you — a retirement date, an income floor, a purchase, protecting your family if something happens to you? We put that into plain, specific terms before anything else happens.

2. Where you are now

Next, a factual snapshot of where things stand today: current accounts (RRSP, TFSA, non-registered, employer plans), existing insurance coverage, and cash flow. Nothing is assumed or estimated — bring statements and policy summaries, and we work from what's actually there.

3. How we close the gap

With both of those in hand, you get a specific, written recommendation that connects where you are to where you want to be. It's yours to keep. Whether you act on it with us, act on it elsewhere, or don't act on it at all is entirely your call — the plan doesn't come with strings attached.

What to bring to the first conversation

  • A rough idea of your goal and timeline — exact numbers aren't necessary yet
  • Recent statements for any investment or registered accounts
  • Summaries of any existing life or health insurance coverage
  • Questions — this conversation is as much yours to steer as ours
